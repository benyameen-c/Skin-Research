### One engineering task

**Measure how repeatably a phone can re-find and re-measure a 2 to 5 mm dot.**

- Print a matte target with 2, 3 and 5 mm light dots on a mid-tone background, a millimetre scale and a grey reference in the same plane.
- Take 10 photographs with the phone fully repositioned each time. Repeat on a second day and, if possible, a second phone.
- Report two numbers per dot size: how far the registered outline lands from the true centre (mm), and the spread of measured contrast (% of baseline).
- Compare with the simulation's prediction. Done means a results CSV in the same column layout as `alignment_results.csv` and the capture settings used.

No skin and no patient images are involved.

### One biology question

**In biopsies of a white spot and nearby normal skin from the same person, are the pigment cells in the spot missing, or present but holding pigment they are not passing to neighbouring cells?**

The two answers point to different repairs. If cells are missing, a treatment has to bring cells in. If cells are present, it has to restart delivery. Anyone with paired sections stained for melanocytes and for melanin could answer this by counting within each patient.
