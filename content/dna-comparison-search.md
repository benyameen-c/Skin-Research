## Spot DNA comparison search (module 5, leads only)

### What this page is

<p><span class="tag human">Leads only</span> Nothing here is in the evidence ledger, and nothing was extracted or verified. Statements below repeat what an abstract literally says. It is research, not medical advice. A more full study can be performed by professionals.</p>

Question searched on 28 November 2026: has anyone compared the DNA of a hypopigmented or sun-damaged skin lesion (somatic mutations, mutation burden, copy number, telomere length) with normal-looking skin next to it in the same person, or sequenced single melanocytes or clones from sun-exposed skin?

Logged in `data/search_log.csv` (rows marked "spot DNA comparison search"): 6 PubMed queries and 2 Europe PMC full-text queries. One screening line per record in `data/screening_log.csv` (`found_by` = `dna_comparison_search`). The earlier damage scan (PR 31) already covered somatic mutation burden in normal sun-exposed skin and single-melanocyte genomes (PMIDs 33029006, 42045571, 25999502 and others); those were not repeated and are not listed here.

### Headline

- **No study was found that compares DNA inside an IGH spot, or any acquired small white spot, with the skin next to it.** The Europe PMC full-text search on "guttate hypomelanosis" with sequencing, somatic mutation, telomere length or copy number terms returned no such paper.
- **The closest white-spot designs are in vitiligo**, which is an immune disease and not IGH: telomerase activity with biopsies "one from lesional and another from nonlesional skin" (PMID 30706515), DNA methylation using "pairwise comparisons of lesions, peri-lesions, and healthy skin" (PMID 38182908), and RNA sequencing of lesional and nonlesional skin (PMID 40513622).
- **Sun-exposed versus sun-protected skin of the same person is a well-worn design for two DNA markers**: mitochondrial DNA deletions (about 15 leads, 1994 to 2026) and telomere length (2 leads).

[Inference: a same-person spot-versus-adjacent comparison of mitochondrial DNA deletions or telomere length in IGH would be new, and both assays already have published protocols in sun-exposed human skin.]

### What was searched and not found

- Single-cell or clonal melanocyte sequencing of sun-exposed skin beyond what PR 31 already lists: the dedicated query found 12 new records and none sequenced melanocytes from a white spot.
- Copy-number studies in non-tumour sun-damaged skin: the copy-number hits were tumours (excluded).
